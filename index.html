Instruction

HOW TO PLAY

You are the last lantern-keeper. You do not swing a sword. You endure.

Movement

WASD or arrow keys. On touch, press anywhere and drag: a floating joystick appears. Weapons fire automatically. Esc/P pause. M mute. 123 pick a draft card, R reroll.

The Loop

  • Kill the Hollow. Walk over the gold shards they leave to gain XP.
  • Every level freezes time and offers three relics: new weapons, weapon ranks, new passives, passive ranks, or golden evolutions.
  • You may carry at most 4 weapons and 4 passives. Each caps at level 5.
  • Weapons at level 5 evolve if you carry their paired passive — the golden card.
  • Elites every 1.5–2 minutes drop gold and a reliquary chest that opens a free draft.
  • Bosses arrive at 5:00, 10:00 and 15:00. THE FINAL BELL arrives at 20:00. Kill it to win.

Evolutions

Survival Notes

  • You have 0.4s of invulnerability after any hit. Armor reduces each hit.
  • Boss attacks flash and wind up before they fire. Watch the ring.
  • The long dark begins at 13:00 — spawn pressure rises 1.6× until 20:00.
  • Untouched kill streaks past 25 are the closest thing to a score multiplier this void offers.
